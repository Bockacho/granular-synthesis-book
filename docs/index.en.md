# Granular Synthesis

<p class="lead" markdown="span">From the first grain to playing live. One sound, a teaspoon hitting a cup, runs through the whole book, and by the end it grows into [twenty very different instruments](chast-4-praktika/index.md).</p>

[Start reading](kak-chitat.md){ .md-button .md-button--primary }
[Sound map](chast-4-praktika/13-karta-zvuka.md){ .md-button }

</div>

![One sound: whole, cut into pieces and scattered as a cloud of grains](images/1-1.png)

</div>

The main text is clear to a twelve-year-old, and the "Under the hood" boxes give the physics and formulas to those who want to go deeper. Three instruments are covered: Xfer Serum 2, Ableton Granulator III and Arturia Pigments. Chapters 2, 5, 6 and 7 have sound demos: move a knob and hear right away what it does.
